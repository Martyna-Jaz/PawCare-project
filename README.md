# Paw Care – Dog Care Website

## Business Overview
- **Business Name:** Paw Care
- **Services Offered:** Dog washing & cleaning, daily dog walking, and playtime and pet entartainment.
- **Target Audience:** Busy dog owners who lack time during the day to exercise, play, clean, and wash their dogs enough.
- **What Makes Us Different:** Run by passionate students, affordable, flexible scheduling, and an overall caring approach to hygiene, exercise and play.

## Color Palette (HEX Codes)
- **Dark Coffee (`#3A2512`):** Primary text, footer, and Call-to-Action buttons
- **Thistle (`#E7CBF6`):** Secondary card borders and section dividers
- **Frosted Blue (`#BCEFFB`):** Hero banner background and list accents
- **Lavender Veil (`#F4DEEC`):** Main page background

## User Story
As a busy dog owner, I want to easily view your washing, walking, and playtime services and click to book an appointment, so that my dog stays clean, active, and happy while I am busy.

## Review task
- Start on `index.html`, then open `services.html` and choose a service.
- Complete the booking form in `booking.html` with fictional details.
- Submitting the form downloads a CSV record in the browser. This static demo does not transmit or store bookings on a server.
